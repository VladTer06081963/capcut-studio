# Episode 01: Infiltration

**Series:** Cyber Heist
**Genre:** Neo-punk, Cyberpunk, Action, Thriller

## Premise
In the neon-lit megacity of Neo-Kowloon, a skilled hacker named "Kael" infiltrates a highly secure corporate data center to steal a prototype AI core.

## Scenes
1. **Establishing Shot:** A sprawling, rain-slicked neo-punk city at night. Gritty streets, neon signs reflecting in the puddles, intense cyberpunk atmosphere.
2. **Action:** Kael, wearing a high-tech augmented reality visor and a dark trench coat, types furiously on a holographic keyboard in a dark alleyway.
3. **Action:** A heavy blast door slowly grinds open, revealing a sterile, brightly lit server room with glowing blue data pillars.
4. **Action:** Kael sneaks past a patrolling security drone, moving silently between the server racks.
5. **Climax:** Kael rips a glowing red AI core from the central console. Alarms instantly trigger, bathing the room in flashing red light.
6. **Action:** Kael sprints down a hallway, dodging laser fire from automated turrets, clutching the core.
7. **Ending:** Kael dives out of a window into the rainy night, a grappling hook firing from his wrist.

## Voiceover
Kael: "They say corporate data is more valuable than human life. Tonight, we find out if they're right."
