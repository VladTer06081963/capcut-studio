# Cyber Heist

**Genre:** Cyberpunk, Action, Thriller

## Visual Style
Neon-lit, dark, gritty, high-contrast. Colors are predominantly cyan, magenta, and deep black. Rain is constant.

## Cast

### Kael
- **Role:** Hacker/Infiltrator
- **Appearance:** 25 years old. Wears a high-tech augmented reality visor that covers his eyes (glowing blue). Dark trench coat, tactical boots.
- **Vibe:** Focused, agile, silent.

## Settings

### Neo-Kowloon
A sprawling megacity. Dense skyscrapers, flying vehicles, holographic billboards.

### Corp Data Center
Sterile, brightly lit with blue LEDs. Endless rows of server racks. Highly secure.
